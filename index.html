About

Hi, I'm Adi Nubli.

I run Akent, a social media management agency built around one idea: local F&B businesses deserve social content that actually sounds like them, not a template. I handle everything from day-to-day posting to campaign planning, working closely with each brand to find a voice that fits how they already talk to their customers.
